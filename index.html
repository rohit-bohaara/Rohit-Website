It started with one question.

How can a single frame hold a feeling, a place, or a moment that will never happen again?

That curiosity turned into practice. I learned light, then composition, then patience. Every shoot taught me something a tutorial could not, mostly about how to look.

Today I shoot portraits, events, lifestyle and cinematic film across Mumbai. I am after images that feel natural, emotional and timeless, not a record of who stood where.

I am still learning with every project, and I plan to keep it that way.

Telling stories, one frame at a time.
